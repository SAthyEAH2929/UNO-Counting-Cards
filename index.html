📖 Game Rules: 46/100/200 Modes, Traps & Ludo Knockouts

1. UNO Draw: Draw 1 card on your turn. Advance your runner forward by the card value (0 = 10 steps, +2 = 2 steps, +4 Wild = 4 steps).

2. 🕳️ Pitfall Trap: Stepping into a pitfall sends your runner all the way back to START (Tile 1)!

3. 🪤 Freeze Trap: Stepping into a freeze trap makes you miss your next card draw turn!

4. 💥 Ludo Knockout: Landing on another player's tile eliminates them back to START!

5. 🪜 Ladders: Climb up shortcut ladders to get ahead!
