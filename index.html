📖 Game Rules: UNO Movement, Ladders & Ludo Knockout

1. UNO Draw Instead of Dice: On your turn, draw 1 card. Move your pawn forward by the card's number (0 = 10 steps, +2 = 2 steps, +4 Wild = 4 steps).

2. Action Cards: SKIP skips next player; REVERSE reverses turn order; SWAP exchanges pawn position with the leader!

3. 💥 Ludo Knockout (Kill Rule): If your pawn lands on a tile occupied by another player, you knock them out back to START (Tile 1) and gain +2 points!

4. 🪜 Ladders: Land on a ladder base (Tiles 7, 12, 24, 37) to climb straight up to higher tiles!

5. 🏆 Victory: First player to reach or enter FINISH (Tile 46) wins the game!
