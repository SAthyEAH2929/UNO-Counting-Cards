📖 Game Rules & Strategy Guide
Mechanic How it works
🏁 Opening Draw Each player chooses Rock, Paper, or Scissors. If a gesture beats the other, that player starts. Ties are resolved randomly.
🔢 Circular Counting The game begins with an initial count of 6. Active cards are counted from left to right (row by row), wrapping around infinitely. Removed cards are automatically skipped.
🎯 Landing & Points The card where the count ends is revealed and removed from the board. The active player scores +1 point. That card's number dictates the count for the next turn.
0️⃣ Zero Card Landing on 0 sets the next count to 10 (or 1, depending on the host's table rules).
⚡ SKIP Card Maintains the current count and skips the next player's turn entirely.
🔄 SWAP Card Maintains the current count. After gaining the point, swaps total scores with the next player in turn order!
🏆 Winning The game ends when all cards are cleared from the board. The player with the highest score wins!
