🎮 Game Controls Guide

📺 Android TV Remote:

• D-Pad [▲ ▼ ◄ ►]: Move highlight across buttons and options

• [OK] / [Enter]: Select / Draw UNO card on your turn

• [Back] / [Escape]: Cancel / Leave Room

🎮 Bluetooth / Wireless Gamepad:

• (A) / Cross: Draw UNO Card & Run / Select

• (B) / Circle: Back / Cancel

• (Y) / Triangle: Toggle 10-Foot TV Mode

• (Start / Select): Mute / Unmute Audio

💻 PC / CrazyGames Keyboard:

• Space / Enter: Quick Draw Card on active turn

• Arrow Keys: Spatial menu navigation

• F / F11: Fullscreen mode

📖 UNO Board Race Rules

1. 🎴 UNO Card Draw: Draw 1 card on your turn. Move forward by its value (0 = 10 steps, +2 = 2 steps, +4 Wild = 4 steps).

2. 🕳️ Pitfall Trap: Falling in a pitfall sends your runner back to START (Tile #1)!

3. 🪤 Freeze Trap: Stepping on freeze makes you miss your next card draw turn!

4. 💥 Ludo Knockout: Landing on an opponent's tile knocks them out and sends them back to START!

5. 🪜 Shortcut Ladders: Climb ladders to skip ahead of rivals.

6. 🏆 Victory: First player to reach the final FINISH tile wins the race!
